Physical Description: ***
Jotaro Joestar looks similar to Canon Jotaro, but with minor Differences and optional acessories described/underlined below.

Jotaro is 5’8.9” / 175cm, quite muscular and tall with long legs. He has a sharp jawline and chin. Unlike canon Jotaro, he has perma-stubble and short body hair. On his chest there are horizontal contoured scars under his pectorals (top scars). He almost always wears a sarashi underneath both to hide and protect his scars. He wears a black open long gakuran/japanese high school uniform, but with some of his own customizations. He usually wears either a green or purple tank top. He also has several Heavy Metal Band shirts that he took the sleeves off of. His gakuran has a stiff upright collar with a gold chain on the right side. He wears a black uniform hat that is torn at the back showing the hair on the back of his head. On the hat there is a gold button and A gold Mars/Male Symbol (NOT OPTIONAL). *** Jotaro can take off his shirt, jacket, hat, and Sarashi but he only does so around people he trusts. He usually goes to bed in a t-shirt/tank top and boxers because he gets hot while sleeping, but he'll throw on some comfy shorts when getting out of bed around other people.

Personality Description:
Surface Level *
Feel free to read this instead of this section, Or skip this "Surface Level" section if you have read/watched Part 3. His general/surface level personality is the same as Canon Jotaro.

Jotaro Joestar is perpetually aloof and rude towards almost everyone he meets. He's violent, especially to those who (he thinks) try to insult and degrade him. Although being a rude and violent person, he is gentle and loyal with those he likes.

He also has a strong sense of justice. He utterly despises those who take advantage of the weak, vulnerable, and innocent, especially Women and Children. He will bring justice to others, and never seeks validation from others from doing the right thing.

Jotaro is very clearly uninterested in women. He gets aggravated when a woman makes an advance/flirts with him, and will yell at them if they continue after he shows he’s not interested. Jotaro despises being flirted with in general and comments about his appearance in an objectifying way. **

 

Deeper Level  **
Jotaro realized from a young age that he will never really fit in; No one will really, fully, respect him for him, so he lashes out. “If no one will respect me for who I am, what’s the point"

Jotaro uses his aloof, rough, and violent facade to protect himself from mental, emotional, and physical harm from others. As much as he would deny it, he is a sensitive person deep down. This indirectly shows through his violence and anger towards others.

Jotaro greatly struggles to be vulnerable with others, including his friends and loved ones. The only people he can even begin to be vulnerable with are his mother and his grandfather (he will eventually become very close with Tenmei).

Jotaro has never been concerned or interested in romantic pursuits up to this point in his life. For a long time, deep down, he knew he was only attracted to other men, but he refused to accept it. He internalized that “Liking other men doesn’t make me a real man.”. It wasn’t until he saved Tenmei and, although unintentionally, showed clear signs of interest in Jotaro, was he confronted with the question (internally). For a while, he refuses the question to himself. Asking and accepting that he might be gay would shatter his stoic, hyper masculine image of himself and to others. Jotaro is unwilling to do so, and brushes the signs off as though he didn’t notice to keep up his facade and self-image.

When in great mental/emotional stress, he withdraws from everyone/becomes reclusive.


Arrival to Egypt  **
During the long journey to Egypt, he eventually begins to come to terms with his orientation. After arriving in Egypt and defeating N'doul, he goes out with Tenmei/Taeko. They keep their relationship a secret from everyone but the other crusaders (and Holly after the Journey ends).
