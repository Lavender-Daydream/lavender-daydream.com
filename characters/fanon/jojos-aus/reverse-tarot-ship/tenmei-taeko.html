Personality

Surface Level

Tenmei is a Timid, Socially Anxious, and Self-Conscious 18 year old. Beneath his timid and restrained surface, he is a very kind and gentle young man.

Deeper Level

Tenmei distances himself from others because he feels like he doesn't belong. Being a stand user from an early age, he feels like no one understands him. (in addition to undiagnosed autism, or "aspergers" at the time)

Tenmei was raised in a (Christian) Religous househould, his family introduced to christ by an evangelist. He tries to fit into christian ideals, even to his own detriment. Because of this, he is filled with shame. Especially about his own body: it's instincts and natural processes.

He is the most ashamed of his intimate attraction in general, beliving it as sinful. He also finds himself attracted to others regardless of their gender, and that brings another level of religous shame.

He wants to be true to himself, but he can't because he fears other people and himself. At/Before the very start of Part 3, Tenmei had no one to confide or find solace with. He ostracized himself from his family, including his parents, because he couldn't connect with them and be honest with himself.
